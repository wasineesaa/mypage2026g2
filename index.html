   I created this website while studying Full Stack Web Development. I am currently a second-year Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence student, and this is one of the first websites I have been able to create on my own. Today, I want to share my experience of living in 2026, especially from the perspective of technology, which is developing incredibly fast. We have reached a point where we can create a website within just a few minutes simply by prompting AI. Whether it is creating a website, writing code, fixing errors, or adding new features, AI can help us with many things, and I have personally tried many of them. Even though I sometimes feel that I started learning coding late, I believe that “Starting late is better than never starting at all.” If you have a dream or an interest in creating websites or learning to code, don’t wait until you feel ready, because we never know when we will truly feel ready. I also started when I wasn’t ready and used AI as a learning assistant. Although this is one of the first websites I have created by myself, my very first project was “TODAY IS MENU” also known in Thai as “วันนี้กินอะไรดี?” It came from a simple problem that many people, including myself, have experienced: “What should I eat today?” So I created a website that randomly suggests meals. If you would like to try it, I will leave the link below.

   For me, AI is extremely useful, especially as a student studying technology. However, I believe we should use it mindfully—not to let it do everything for us, but to help reduce our workload. When using AI to write code, we should still understand the basic principles and be able to explain what is happening behind our website. If we own a website but have no idea how it actually works, I think that would be a shame, and we might unknowingly be limiting our own ability to learn. Everything I have shared here is simply the personal experience of one student living in 2026. Oh, and I almost forgot to mention: I originally wrote this text in Thai and then used AI to translate it into English. I guess that is another great advantage of AI—it can help us communicate more easily with people who speak different languages. So, everything I have shared is based on my personal experience, and I encourage you to use your own judgment when taking these ideas and applying them to your life.
